# Recursos (`recursos/`)

Material the teacher has **reviewed and approved for use in class**: internet resources, e-books and pedagogical guides, organized by area (`recursos/fisica/`, `recursos/matematicas/`, `recursos/algebra/`, …). If something is in `recursos/`, you may use it without asking.

- **What they're for:** the source of *content ideas* — examples, exercises, problem contexts, activity sequences, explanations, lab ideas. When planning a period and writing any guía, clase, taller, quiz or tarea, look here **before** inventing material.
- **The DBA still decide *what*; the recursos inspire *how*.** Use only the parts that fit the course's component and the planned temas (e.g. the math modules cover every component: take the geometry parts for Geometría, the numeric-variational parts for Álgebra/Cálculo). If a recurso contradicts the DBA scope or has a math error, the DBA and correct math win — tell the teacher about the error; approved doesn't mean infallible.
- **Adapt, don't paste.** Rewrite in our templates and our voice, with the grade's register (`.claude/referencias/registro-grados.md`), black-and-white graphics and Colombian contexts. When an example, exercise or activity comes from a recurso, credit it: a `\bibitem` in the guía's referencias, and a comment `% Recurso: recursos/<ruta>` in clase/taller/quiz/tarea.
- **Reading them:** each `.docx` collection has a **`markdown/`** folder next to it — read and search those first (`grep -ril "inecuaci" recursos/*/*/markdown/`). They're made by `tools/docx_a_markdown.py` and keep the formulas as LaTeX (`$…$` inline, a ```` ```latex ```` block when a formula stands alone), the numbering of exercises («5.», «b.») so an exercise can be cited, headings and tables. **Most formulas in the math *Guías de Apoyo* are Microsoft Equation 3.0 objects** (710 in the 11° module); the tool decodes their MTEF data (4 581 of 4 587 in the math modules; the 6 it can't read show as `\text{[ecuación ilegible]}`), and the physics guides use modern Word equations (OMML). Each Equation 3.0 object also carries a spoken alt text («x sobre 4 menor o igual que 70»); when its numbers don't match the formula's, the tool leaves it next to it as `<!-- alt: … -->` — often only a wording difference, sometimes a typo in the recurso (the printed formula is what students see). The older plain-text copies in `texto/` (`tools/docx_a_texto.py`) mark every Equation 3.0 formula as «[ecuación]»; use them only where `markdown/` is missing. Open the original `.docx` when a figure matters. `recursos/algebra/baldor/baldor.tex` is a LaTeX transcription of Baldor's *Álgebra* — search the `.tex`.
- **Adding resources:** only the teacher adds here. You may *suggest* an internet resource, but it only counts as approved once she saves it here. When she adds a `.docx`, create its copies: `python3 tools/docx_a_markdown.py <archivo>.docx` (→ `markdown/`, formulas as LaTeX; standard library only, works anywhere; add `--diagnostico` to list anything it couldn't translate) and `python3 tools/docx_a_texto.py <archivo>.docx` (→ `texto/`; `textutil -convert txt` is macOS-only and drops formulas silently). For a `.pdf`, `pdftotext -layout` into `texto/`. Never modify the originals.
- `guias-anteriores/` (repo root) is **not** a recurso: it's previous-year work kept for reference (its "DBA:" fields are not official).

## Banco de ejercicios (`recursos/banco/`)

Exercises verified with Python + SymPy, one file per tema (`recursos/banco/<area>/<tema>.py`),
state in `recursos/banco/verificados.json`. It's the one part of `recursos/` the agent writes:
through the `verificar-ejercicios` skill only, and an exercise counts as approved material
only once its check passes (or, for open-ended ones, once the teacher approves the model
answer). Look here first for exercises: `python3 tools/ejercicios.py listar --tema … --grado …`.
What's still pending before the bank can be fully trusted is in `recursos/banco/PENDIENTES.md`.

| `matematicas/icfes/` | Official ICFES material (icfes.gov.co), saved 2026-09-13: the *Cuadernillo de preguntas* de Matemáticas Saber 11.º (marzo 2026; 50 released questions, selección múltiple, with the answer table and the afirmación of each question at the end) and the *Guía de orientación Saber 11.º 2026-1, Calendario B* (competencias 34 % interpretación y representación · 43 % formulación y ejecución · 23 % argumentación; contenidos genéricos/no genéricos for Álgebra y cálculo, Geometría, Estadística). | `algebra/baldor/` | Partial LaTeX transcription (17 pp.) of the first chapters of Baldor, *Álgebra* (1983): nomenclatura, clasificación de expresiones, axiomas de los reales, signos de agrupación, multiplicación, leyes de signos, exponentes y coeficientes — each exercise cites the original page. Uses decimal point, not comma. | Exercise bank for Álgebra 8° (operaciones con expresiones algebraicas). It has no inecuaciones or factorización yet. |
